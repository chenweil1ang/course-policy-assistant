You can ask this assistant about the module's coursework deadlines, assessment weightings, submission method, and Coursework 1 feedback; current late-submission penalties; medical extension eligibility and application steps; academic integrity, citations, collusion, and contract cheating; and the teaching timetable, laboratories, Room 217, and office hours.

The assistant answers from current passages in its local corpus. Questions outside these topics may not be answerable. Superseded passages are retained for reference but are not current policy.
